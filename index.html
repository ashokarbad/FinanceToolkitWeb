Smart capture

The boring part, automated.

No more typing expenses one by one. Scan a receipt, import a statement — the app does the categorising. On-device. No cloud.

Receipt Scanner

Snap. Done. Categorised.

Photograph a bill or pick from library. OCR extracts the total, merchant, and date — then auto-sorts into 27 categories.

  • Smart total extraction with keyword detection (total, amount due, grand total) and last-match strategy for multi-total receipts
  • OCR misread correction — strips stray characters when ₹ is read as 7, 2, etc.
  • Phone number and GST filtering to avoid false amount detection
  • 12 currency symbols supported
Bank Statement Import

Months of expenses, in seconds.

Import a PDF or CSV bank statement. UPI, NEFT, IMPS, ECS — every transaction auto-categorised, ready to select and add.

  • AI category engine with 300+ keywords across 27 categories
  • Learns from your corrections — category memory improves with use
  • PDF parsing via on-device PDFKit, never sent to a server
  • Select what to import, skip what you don't
